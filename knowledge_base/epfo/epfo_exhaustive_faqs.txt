### Category: General / Default
Q: Whether an employer can deduct employer’s share of contribution from the wages of employees?
A: No. It is not permissible. Any such deduction is a criminal offence.

Q: Can the wages be reduced by the employer on account of payment to the EPF?
A: No. Under Section 12 of the Act, it is prohibited.

Q: Whether a daily rated employee or the piece rated employee can become a member of the EPF?
A: Yes, any employee receiving wages directly or indirectly from any employer is coverable.

Q: If an employee is paid wages on daily basis or on piece rate basis how the contribution is determined?
A: Contributions are determined on the total wages earned during the month, subject to the statutory limit.

Q: Whether the member is entitled for full interest on the belated deposit of PF dues by the employer?
A: Yes, the member gets full interest for the entire period as per the annual declared rate.

Q: Can an employee contribute to the EPF after leaving the service?
A: No, contributions are payable only during employment in a covered establishment.

Q: Whether an employee can become a member of EPF without any age restriction?
A: Yes, there is no age restriction for becoming a member.

Q: Whether an apprentice can become a member of the EPF?
A: No, apprentices under the Apprentices Act are excluded.

Q: If an employee is transferred, whether he is required to be enrolled as a member once again?
A: No, but his UAN must be linked by the new employer.

Q: Whether an employee can continue as a PF member even after retirement?
A: Yes, if he continues to work in a covered establishment.

### Category: Claim & Withdrawals / TDS on Claims
Q: At what rate TDS will be deducted if PF account is linked with a valid PAN for resident member?
A: 10 percent (Section 194 A).

Q: At what rate TDS will be deducted if PAN is not linked/invalid?
A: 20 percent.

Q: Is there any minimum amount up to which tax is not deducted?
A: Yes, currently Rs. 50,000/- for PF withdrawals (Section 192A).

Q: Under which circumstances TDS will be applicable?
A: Final Settlement, transfer claims, and certain accounting processes after year 2021-22.

### Category: Others / Pension Scheme (EPS-1995)
Q: Whether an employee who has not opted for the earlier Family Pension Scheme, 1971 can join the EPS-1995?
A: Yes.

Q: What are the advantages of taking a Scheme Certificate?
A: It facilitates transfer of Pension Accounts and ensures family pension in case of the holder's death.

Q: At what age a member is eligible for pension?
A: Minimum 58 years for superannuation pension; 50 years for early pension (with reduction).

Q: How long can one continue EPF membership?
A: As long as one is in service in a covered establishment.

### Category: Others / UAN & KYC
Q: Is it compulsory to change Husband’s name in place of Father’s name in UAN after marriage?
A: No, it is not mandatory, but UAN details should match the Aadhaar for seamless services.

Q: Can we generate UAN for Nepal Based Employees without Aadhaar?
A: Yes, using other valid documents like Passport.
