# Page 5: Section 139(9) – Defective Return Notice

**Page ID:** SECTION_139_9_NOTICE
**Category:** TAX_NOTICES

## Official Sources
- Defective Return Rules: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/communications/notification/notification-62-2023
- 139(9) Guidelines: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/notices/139-9

## Notice Details
- **Definition:** Notice issued when the ITR is considered incomplete or contains obvious errors specified in the law.
- **Legal Reference:** Section 139(9) of Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Issued By:** Centralized Processing Center (CPC) or Assessing Officer (AO).

## Common Reasons for Defects
- Missing mandatory schedules (e.g., Balance Sheet/P&L for businesses).
- Taxes paid but not reported (TDS/Self-Assessment Tax).
- Arithmetic errors in computation.
- Unsigned return/unverified return.
- Missing specific fields in the ITR form.
- Incomplete audit reports (if required).

## How to Correct
- **Deadline:** Within **15 days** from notice receipt (can be extended).
- **Actions:**
  - **Online Response:** Login to portal → 'Pending Actions' → 'e-Proceedings' → 'Submit Response'.
  - **Correction:** Modify the ITR and resubmit as a "Response to Section 139(9)".

## Consequences of Non-Response
- The return will be rejected and treated as **INVALID (Never Filed)**.
- Penalties for late filing apply.
- No processing of tax credits or refunds.
- Potential interest under Section 234A.
