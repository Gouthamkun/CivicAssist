# Page 4: Section 143(1) Intimation Notice

**Page ID:** SECTION_143_1_NOTICE

## Notice Details
- **Definition:** Automated intimation sent after return processing (matching software-calculated tax vs. taxpayer-filed tax).
- **Legal Reference:** Section 143(1) of Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Nature:** Preliminary assessment communication (not a scrutiny or audit notice).
- **Issuance Timeline:** Within 1 year from the end of the financial year in which the return is filed.

## Three Possible Outcomes
- **No Demand / No Refund:** Calculations match perfectly. No action needed.
- **Refund Due:** Tax paid > Tax liability. Refund will be credited.
- **Tax Demand:** Discrepancies found resulting in extra tax. Pay or contest.

## Common Reasons for Notice
- Tax mismatch in return vs AIS / Form 26AS.
- Arithmetical errors in computation.
- Internal inconsistencies in the ITR sections.
- Mismatch in TDS/TCS claims.
- Disallowed deductions or exemptions (e.g., Chapter VI-A).

## Required Action
- **Deadline:** Within **30 days** from the date of the notice.
- **Options:**
  - Accept and pay the demand (via e-pay tax).
  - Submit an online response/explanation via 'e-Proceedings'.
  - File Rectification Request under Section 154 for clerical errors.
  - File Revised Return if the deadline has not passed.
