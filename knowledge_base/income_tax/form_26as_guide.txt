# Page 8: Form 26AS (Tax Credit Statement)

**Page ID:** FORM_26AS
**Category:** STATEMENTS

## Official Sources
- Form 26AS Guide: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/communications/faqs-form-26as
- Portal Access: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/login
- Technical Specs: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/communications/notification/notification-43-2023

## Overview
- **Definition:** Consolidated annual tax statement showing all TDS/TCS deducted and paid on your behalf.
- **Legal Basis:** Section 203A(1) of Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Issued By:** Income Tax Department (TRACES portal).

## Contents of Form 26AS
- **TDS:** Details of Tax Deducted at Source by employer (Sec 192), Banks (Sec 194A), or others.
- **TCS:** Tax Collected at Source on high-value items (e.g., car purchase).
- **Advance/Self-Assessment Tax:** Record of taxes you paid directly via challans.
- **Tax Refunds:** Details of refunds received during the year (with interest).
- **High-Value Transactions (SFT):** Transactions reported by banks/financial institutions (Property, Mutual Funds).
- **Turnover details:** From GSTR-3B filings (if applicable).

## How to Access
1. Visit [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in) and login.
2. Navigate to **'e-File'** → **'Income Tax Returns'** → **'View Form 26AS'**.
3. Redirect to TRACES portal → Agree to terms → 'View Tax Credit'.
4. Select Financial Year → View as HTML → Export as PDF.

## How to Use while Filing ITR
- **Validation:** Match TDS amounts in Form 26AS with your Form 16/16A.
- **Credit Claim:** Ensure all TDS shown in 26AS is correctly entered in the ITR "Schedules for Tax Credit".
- **Refund Claim:** Use 26AS as primary proof when claiming high tax refunds.

## Common Mismatches
- **TDS Missing:** Deductor has not filed the TDS return. Contact them to file/update.
- **Wrong Amount:** Deductor filed the wrong PAN or amount. Request a corrected Form 16A/ITR.
- **Timeline:** TDS usually reflects in 26AS within 1-3 months after the end of the quarter.
