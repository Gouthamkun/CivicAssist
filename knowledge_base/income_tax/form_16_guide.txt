# Page 10: Form 16 – Salary TDS Certificate

**Page ID:** FORM_16
**Category:** STATEMENTS

## Official Sources
- Form 16 Guide: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/communications/faqs-form-16

- Employer Resources: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/employers

## Overview
- **Definition:** A certificate issued by an employer to its employees, documenting the total salary paid and tax deducted (TDS) during the financial year.
- **Legal Basis:** Section 203A(2) of Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Deadline:** Must be issued by **31st May** every year for the previous FY.

## Components of Form 16
- **Part A:** Summary of TDS deposited by the employer. Includes Employer TAN, PAN, and Employee PAN. Verified via the TRACES portal.
- **Part B (Annexure):** Detailed breakup of:
  - Gross Salary (Basic, HRA, LTA, Conveyance).
  - Exemptions under Section 10 (HRA, LTA, etc.).
  - Deductions under Chapter VI-A (80C, 80D, etc.).
  - Final Taxable Income and Tax Payable/Refund.

## How to Use while Filing ITR
- **Schedule S (Salary):** Directly copy values from Form 16 Part B (Gross Salary, Allowances, Deductions).
- **Schedule IT (Tax Payments):** Enter the TDS amount from Form 16 Part A and cross-check with Form 26AS.

## Obtaining Form 16
- **Direct:** From HR/Accounts department of your employer.
- **Missing:** If your employer didn't provide it, check Form 26AS for TDS entries. Use salary slips to compute income.
