# Page 9: AIS – Annual Information Statement

**Page ID:** AIS_STATEMENT
**Category:** STATEMENTS

## Overview
- **Definition:** Comprehensive statement showing all high-value financial transactions reported by third parties (Banks, Brokers, Registrar, etc.) linked to your PAN.
- **Purpose:** To increase tax transparency and reduce underreporting of income.
- **Relation to 26AS:** AIS is a much broader statement. While 26AS focus on Taxes, AIS includes all major financial activities regardless of whether tax was deducted.

## AIS Contents
- **Salary Income:** Reported by your employer.
- **Interest Income:** From Savings Accounts and Fixed Deposits (Reported by Banks).
- **Dividends:** Reported by companies and mutual funds.
- **Securities Transactions:** Buying and Selling of stocks/mutual funds (Reported by Brokers).
- **Real Estate:** Buying or Selling of property (Reported by Sub-registrar).
- **Foreign Remittances:** Money sent or received from abroad.
- **Crypto:** Transactions on crypto exchanges (if reported).
- **Credit Card Payments:** High-value credit card bill payments (> ₹10 lakh).

## TIS (Taxpayer Information Summary)
- TIS is a consolidated summary of AIS.
- It shows "Reported Value" and "Derived Value" (your feedback value).
- **Recommendation:** Use TIS values for pre-filling your ITR for accuracy.

## Feedback Mechanism
- You can submit feedback on any transaction shown in AIS:
  - 'Information is correct'
  - 'Information is not taxable' (e.g., reimbursement)
  - 'Information is duplicate'
  - 'Information is denied' (not your transaction)
- Accepted feedback updates the TIS values.
