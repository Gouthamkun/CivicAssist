# Page 6: Section 148 – Income Escaping Assessment (Reassessment)

**Page ID:** SECTION_148_NOTICE
**Category:** TAX_NOTICES

## Official Sources
- Section 148 Rules: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/communications/notification/notification-75-2023
- Reassessment Guidelines: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/notices/148

## Notice Details
- **Definition:** Notice issued when the Assessing Officer (AO) has reason to believe that any income chargeable to tax has escaped assessment for any assessment year.
- **Legal Reference:** Section 148 of Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Time Limit:** 
  - Up to **3 years** from the end of the Assessment Year (AY) for normal cases.
  - Up to **10 years** if the escaped income is **₹50 lakh or more**.

## Reasons for Reassessment
- Income was not reported in the original return.
- Income was deliberately suppressed or underreported.
- Expenses were falsely overestimated to reduce tax.
- Wrongful claim of exemptions or deductions.
- New evidence discovered by the department (e.g., from AIS/SFT).
- Income from undisclosed sources (Bank, Cash, Foreign Assets).

## Notice & Response Process
1. **Inquiry (Section 148A):** Mandatory pre-notice inquiry by AO.
2. **Show-Cause (Section 148A(b)):** AO issues a notice asking why reassessment should not be done. Taxpayer must respond within 7-30 days.
3. **Decision (Section 148A(d)):** AO decides whether to proceed. If yes, the Section 148 Notice is issued.
4. **Filing Fresh Return:** The taxpayer is required to file a fresh ITR for the relevant year.
5. **Assessment:** Final reassessment order is issued after reviewing the return and evidence.

## Rights During Reassessment
- Right to be heard and submit evidence.
- Right to request used "Reasons to Believe".
- Right to file formal objections to the notice.
- Right to appeal the final assessment order.
