## Key Deadlines (FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26)
- **Individual / Salaried (Regular):** July 31st, 2025.
- **Audit Cases (Business/Audit):** October 31st, 2025.
- **Transfer Pricing Cases:** November 30th, 2025.
- **Belated / Revised Return:** December 31st, 2025.

## Verification Deadline
- Must e-verify within **30 days** of filing.

## Penalties & Consequences
### Section 234F (Late Filing Fee)
- If filed after the deadline:
  - **Income <= ₹5 lakh:** ₹1,000.
  - **Income > ₹5 lakh:** ₹5,000.
### Section 234A (Interest)
- If tax is unpaid: **1% per month** interest from the deadline until filing.
### Other Consequences
- **Losses:** Business/Capital losses cannot be carried forward to next years.
- **Refunds:** Delay in refund processing and no interest on refund for the delay period.

## Revised vs Belated Return
- **Revised Return:** Filed to correct errors in the original return. No penalty if original was on time.
- **Belated Return:** Filed after the deadline. Corrects the failure to file, but attracts Section 234F penalty.

## Typical Queries
- What is the ITR filing deadline?
- What happens if I file ITR late?
- What are the penalties for late filing?
- Can I file a revised return after the deadline? (Yes, until Dec 31st).
- Can I file ITR after 2 years? (Only via ITR-U with higher penalties).
