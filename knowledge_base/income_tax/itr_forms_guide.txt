## ITR Form Selection Matrix

### ITR-1 (Sahaj)
- **Full Name:** Sahaj
- **Eligible Taxpayers:** Resident individuals (not RNOR/NRIs).
- **Income Conditions:** Total income up to ₹50 lakhs.
- **Income Sources:** Salary/Pension, One House Property, Other Sources (Interest, etc.), Agricultural income up to ₹5,000.
- **Exclusions:** Directors, holders of unlisted equity shares, those with capital gains.

### ITR-2
- **Full Name:** Standard
- **Eligible Taxpayers:** Individuals and HUFs not having business/professional income.
- **Income Conditions:** No limit on total income.
- **Income Sources:** Capital gains (Stocks/Property), Foreign income/assets, more than one house property, Agricultural income over ₹5,000.
- **Applicability:** For those who do not qualify for ITR-1.

### ITR-3
- **Full Name:** Business
- **Eligible Taxpayers:** Individuals and HUFs with income from business or profession.
- **Applicability:** Business owners, freelancers, trade in F&O, partners in firms.

### ITR-4 (Sugam)
- **Full Name:** Sugam
- **Eligible Taxpayers:** Resident individuals, HUFs, and Firms (other than LLP).
- **Condition:** Total income up to ₹50 lakhs under Presumptive Taxation (Sec 44AD/44ADA/44AE).

### ITR-5
- **Eligibility:** LLP, Partnership firms, AOP, BOI, Artificial Juridical Person.

### ITR-6
- **Eligibility:** Companies (other than those claiming exemption under Section 11).

### ITR-7
- **Eligibility:** Persons/Companies including trusts under Section 139(4A/4B/4C/4D).
