## Verification Methods

### Aadhaar OTP
- **Description:** Verify using OTP on mobile registered with Aadhaar.
- **Steps:** Login → 'e-File' → 'Income Tax Returns' → 'e-Verify Return' → Select 'Aadhaar OTP'.
- **Timeline:** 24 hours (usually instant).
- **Documents Needed:** Aadhaar number, Mobile for OTP.

### Bank Account (EVC)
- **Description:** Generate Electronic Verification Code through pre-validated bank account.
- **Eligible Banks:** Most major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.).
- **Timeline:** Immediate.

### Net Banking
- **Description:** Direct verification by logging into the e-filing portal via the bank's net banking page.
- **Timeline:** Immediate.

### Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
- **Description:** Mandatory for audit cases. E-sign using a valid DSC.
- **Timeline:** Immediate.

### Physical Verification (ITR-V)
- **Description:** Send signed paper acknowledgment to CPC, Bengaluru.
- **Steps:** Print ITR-V → Sign in Blue Ink → Send via Speed Post.
- **Timeline:** 30 days from submission.

## Consequences of Non-Verification
- Return will NOT be processed.
- No tax refund will be issued.
- No tax credit available.
- Return treated as completely invalid/not filed.
- May lead to Section 139(8) notice or interest under 234A.
