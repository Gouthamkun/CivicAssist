# Page 7: Responding to Income Tax Notices

**Page ID:** RESPOND_TO_NOTICES
**Category:** TAX_NOTICES

- General Notice Response Guide: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/communications/faqs-notices

## Notice Response Workflow
1. **Identify Notice Type:** Log in to portal → 'Pending Actions' → 'e-Proceedings'. Note the Section (e.g., 143(1), 139(9), 148, 142(1)).
2. **Understand the Query:** Read the notice PDF carefully to identify the specific discrepancy, demand, or missing document.
3. **Gather Documents:** Collect relevant PAN, Aadhaar, Bank Statements, AIS, and Form 16/26AS as per the notice requirement.
4. **File Online Response:**
   - Go to 'Pending Actions' → 'e-Proceedings' → 'View Notice' → 'Submit Response'.
   - Select 'Agree' or 'Disagree' (with remarks).
   - Upload supporting evidence (PDF format, max 5MB).
   - Submit and download the acknowledgment.
5. **Track Status:** Monitor the 'e-Proceedings' section for updates or successful closure.

## Best Practices
- Respond within **15-30 days** as specified in the notice. Do not wait for the deadline.
- Ensure all uploaded documents are clearly legible.
- Keep copies of all digital and physical submissions.
- For complex legal notices like Section 148, consult with a Chartered Accountant (CA).

## Common Consequences of Missing Deadline
- Automatic processing based on department's calculations (best judgment).
- Penalties levied under Section 234F or Section 271/270A.
- Recovery actions initiated for unpaid tax demands.

## Typical Queries
- How do I respond to an income tax notice?
- Where can I see my pending notices?
- What if I miss the response deadline?
- Can I respond to a notice offline? (Online is preferred, but some AO letters require physical reply).
