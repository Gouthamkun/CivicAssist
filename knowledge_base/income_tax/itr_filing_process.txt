## Who Must File ITR
Filing an ITR is mandatory if:
- Income exceeds the basic exemption limit (₹2.5L to ₹5L depending on age).
- You are a company or a firm (regardless of profit/loss).
- You are a resident with foreign assets or signing authority in foreign accounts.
- Professional receipts > ₹10 lakh or Business receipts > ₹60 lakh.
- TDS/TCS > ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
- Foreign travel expenditure > ₹2 lakh.
- Electricity consumption > ₹1 lakh.
- Deposits in current accounts > ₹1 crore.

## Step-by-Step Filing Process
1. **Login:** Login to the e-filing portal using your PAN as User ID.
2. **Navigate:** Go to 'e-File' → 'Income Tax Returns' → 'File Income Tax Return'.
3. **Select Assessment Year:** Choose the relevant year and mode (Online/Offline).
4. **Choose Status:** Select Individual, HUF, etc.
5. **Select ITR Form:** Choose the correct form (ITR-1, ITR-2, etc.).
6. **Select Reason for Filing:** (e.g., taxable income, specific expenditures).
7. **Fill Details:** Validate pre-filled personal, income, and tax-deduction details from AIS/TIS.
8. **Pay Tax:** If there is any liability, pay immediately via 'Pay Now'.
9. **Preview & Submit:** Review all sections and submit.
10. **E-Verify:** Crucial step. Verify using Aadhaar OTP or other methods within 30 days.

## Required Documents
- PAN and Aadhaar (must be linked).
- Bank account details (pre-validated for refund).
- Form 16 (for salaried) or Form 16A (for others).
- Form 26AS and AIS (for tax credit and transaction matching).
- Bank statements and Interest certificates.
- Investment proofs (80C, 80D, etc.).
